# Welcome

## Who we are

FlyBridge Education Group helps international students and working professionals
build a life abroad — one confident step at a time. We offer practical,
self-paced video courses, live seminars, and a student dashboard.

## What the support bot can help with

This bot answers questions about our programs, seminars, pricing, resources and
account features. It can point you to the right course, explain how a program
works, and help you navigate your FlyBridge dashboard.

## How to get started

Start by browsing the Programs page (flybridgeedu.com/programs) to see what is
available today. If you are just exploring, the Student Visa Blueprint is the
most popular starting point for study-abroad plans.

## Contacting a human

If the bot cannot help, reach out through the site's contact channels — the team
replies to every message.
