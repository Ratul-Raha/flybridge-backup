# Seminars

## What seminars are

Seminars are live, expert-led sessions about studying and working abroad. They
are one-off events on the Seminars page (flybridgeedu.com/seminars), separate
from the self-paced video courses.

## Free and paid seminars

Some seminars are free; others are paid and may have a ticket price. Each
seminar card on the Seminars page shows its type, date and time clearly.

## Registering for a seminar

Open the seminar on the Seminars page and register with your name, email and
phone. After registering you get a confirmation email with the details, and the
live link also appears in your FlyBridge dashboard.

## What happens after registration

A registration confirmation email is sent to you automatically. It includes the
date and time, ticket type, and a "Join the seminar" button. The live link also
shows up on the seminar page inside your dashboard.

## Cancellations and missed sessions

If you miss a live session, the seminar page and dashboard are the best places
to look for a replay or notes. The team can also answer questions through the
site's contact channels.
