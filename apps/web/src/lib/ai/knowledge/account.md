# Your Account & Dashboard

## Signing in

Sign in with your Google account from the Login page. If you already registered
for a seminar, use the same Google account so your dashboard shows everything in
one place.

## What's in the dashboard

The dashboard shows your seminar registrations, the live links for sessions you
joined, and access to any programs you purchased. Staff see extra tools for
managing seminars, registrations and notifications.

## Notifications

Registration updates and new announcements appear in the bell dropdown at the
top of the site, and in the dashboard's notifications page. Notifications are
marked as read when you open them.

## Password or access problems

If you cannot sign in, make sure you are using the Google account tied to your
registration. If it still fails, contact the team — do not create a second
account with a different email.
