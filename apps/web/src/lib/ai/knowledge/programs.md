# Programs

## Student Visa Blueprint

The Student Visa Blueprint is a complete step-by-step system to prepare your
study visa application with confidence. It is a paid self-paced video course,
currently sold at an intro price. It covers documents, timelines, and the
application process. The best place to find it is the Programs page — look for
the "Student Visa Blueprint" card and open it to see the current price and
enrolment link.

## English Course — অবচেতন মনে ইংলিশ Level 01

A practical course for confident everyday and interview communication in
English. It is designed for learners who want to speak, not just study grammar.
Available through the Programs page.

## Work Permit Blueprint — Canada

The Work Permit Blueprint for Canada covers eligibility, documentation and
job-search groundwork for working in Canada. It is listed as "upcoming" on the
Programs page, so check there for its release date and enrolment details.

## Interview Readiness

Interview Readiness provides frameworks, mock questions and confident delivery
techniques for job interviews. It is listed as "upcoming" — visit the Programs
page for updates.

## Pricing and payments

Program prices vary by course. Payment details and any intro offers are shown on
each program card on the Programs page. If a price is shown in local currency
(e.g. ৳), that is the intro offer price; the page is always the source of truth.

## How to enrol

Every program card on the Programs page has its own page with enrolment
instructions. If you are logged in, purchased courses appear in your dashboard.
