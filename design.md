# FlyBridge Education Group – Brand & Design Guidelines

## Overview

FlyBridge Education Group is a modern global education platform headquartered in Toronto, Canada, serving primarily students and professionals from Bangladesh, India, and Nepal.

Our mission is not to sell visa services.

Our mission is to help people build an international future through practical education.

FlyBridge should feel like a premium EdTech company—not an immigration consultancy.

---

# Brand Positioning

## We are

- Global Education Platform
- Online Learning Platform
- Career Development Platform
- International Education Ecosystem
- Practical Skill-Based Learning

## We are NOT

- Immigration Agency
- Visa Consultancy
- Overseas Recruiting Agency
- Government Information Portal

The website should communicate confidence, professionalism, accessibility, and modern learning.

---

# Brand Personality

The design should feel:

- Modern
- Premium
- Trustworthy
- Friendly
- International
- Minimal
- Professional
- Optimistic

Avoid making the interface feel overly corporate or luxurious.

Think:

- Coursera
- Stripe
- Linear
- Notion
- Apple

---

# Design Philosophy

The website should inspire users by focusing on outcomes rather than paperwork.

Visitors should immediately feel:

> "This is where ambitious people prepare for global opportunities."

Avoid emphasizing visas, passports, flags, or government processes.

Instead, emphasize learning, growth, careers, and international success.

# Typography

Use clean modern fonts.

Examples:

- Inter
- Geist
- Manrope

Typography should be large and spacious.

Generous white space.

Minimal visual clutter.

---

# Layout Style

Large hero sections

Rounded cards

Soft shadows

Subtle borders

Glass effects only when appropriate

Generous spacing

Modern dashboard-inspired layouts

No heavy gradients

No flashy animations

Animations should feel smooth and intentional.

---

# Photography Style

Use authentic lifestyle photography.

Target audience:

- Bangladeshi students
- Indian students
- Nepalese students

Images should show people who users can relate to.

Examples:

- Students studying
- University campuses
- Libraries
- Modern classrooms
- Coffee shops
- Laptop learning
- Young professionals
- International workplaces

Avoid stock images that feel fake or staged.

---

# Avoid These Images

Do NOT overuse:

✗ Airplanes

✗ Passports

✗ Visa stamps

✗ Immigration counters

✗ Canadian flags everywhere

✗ Statue of Liberty

✗ Government buildings

✗ Handshake stock photos

✗ Smiling consultants in suits

Those visuals make the website feel like a visa agency.

---

# Preferred Visual Themes

Focus on outcomes.

Examples:

- Student studying on laptop
- Student receiving graduation cap
- Friends on university campus
- Young professional working in modern office
- International classroom
- Learning dashboard
- Success celebration
- Career growth

# User Experience Goals

The website should communicate:

- Learning is easy
- The platform is trustworthy
- Courses are practical
- Students can learn independently
- International opportunities are achievable

Navigation should be extremely simple.
