# Easy & Smart Spoken English — Landing Page Design & Development Brief

**Project:** Easy & Smart Spoken English Course  
**Deliverable:** Premium, mobile-first, high-converting course landing page  
**Audience:** Bengali-speaking learners in Bangladesh  
**Primary conversion:** Course enrollment / purchase  
**Secondary conversion:** Course inquiry or support through the designated channel  
**Design philosophy:** Premium education brand × modern learning app × emotionally compelling sales page

---

## 1. The mission

Create a landing page that makes a visitor feel:

1. “This course understands exactly why I struggle to speak English.”
2. “This method feels different from the grammar classes I have tried before.”
3. “I can realistically fit this into my daily routine.”
4. “I can see myself speaking English confidently after completing this course.”
5. “I want to start now.”

The website must not look like a generic educational template, a crowded coaching-center website, or a basic sales funnel.

It should feel like a thoughtfully designed, premium digital learning product.

### The core positioning

**The product is not just an English course. It is a guided transition from understanding English to expressing yourself in English.**

The entire landing page should communicate this transformation.

Primary message:

> ১০০ দিনে হয়ে উঠুন English-এর Boss

Supporting message:

> ইংরেজি বুঝেন, কিন্তু বলতে গেলে আটকে যান?  
> সমস্যা আপনার না, সমস্যা মাথায় অনুবাদ করার অভ্যাসে।

Method:

> Don't Translate, Just Express.

The design should make this method visually understandable, not merely mention it as a slogan.

---

# 2. Creative direction

## Visual identity: Speak with confidence

Create a visual identity inspired by premium SaaS products, modern language-learning apps, and editorial-style education brands.

The page should be:

- Modern, confident, and energetic.
- Premium without looking expensive or intimidating.
- Friendly to beginners.
- Visually rich but never cluttered.
- Highly readable on small screens.
- Conversion-focused without feeling aggressively salesy.

### Recommended visual concept

Use a deep midnight-blue foundation with vibrant lime-green accents, warm off-white surfaces, and expressive typography.

The visual contrast should communicate:

**Before:** Hesitation, confusion, translating in your head.

**After:** Clarity, confidence, natural expression.

Use the transition from a muted visual environment to a bright, energetic one as a recurring design motif.

### Color palette

| Purpose | Color |
|---|---|
| Primary dark | `#101B35` |
| Secondary dark | `#182746` |
| Accent lime | `#C9F36A` |
| Warm background | `#F7F8F3` |
| White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Main text | `#152039` |
| Secondary text | `#667085` |
| Border | `#E5E9E0` |

Use the lime accent for important CTAs, progress indicators, active states, and small visual highlights.

Do not use lime for large blocks of body text.

### Typography

Use a modern, highly readable typeface that supports both Bengali and English.

Recommended:

- Bengali: `Hind Siliguri` or `Noto Sans Bengali`
- English: `Inter` or `Manrope`

Use a consistent typographic system.

| Element | Desktop | Mobile |
|---|---|---|
| Hero headline | 64–76px | 38–44px |
| Section heading | 42–52px | 30–36px |
| Card heading | 22–26px | 19–22px |
| Body text | 17–19px | 15–17px |
| Small label | 12–14px | 11–13px |

Use responsive typography with `clamp()`.

Bengali headlines should have sufficient line-height and natural wrapping. Avoid oversized letter spacing or tightly compressed Bengali text.

The hero must immediately communicate the course promise and encourage the visitor to continue.

Left: Approximately 55% width.

The left side contains the headline, problem statement, supporting copy, CTA, and trust indicators.

Order:

1. Small course label
2. Main headline
3. Supporting problem statement
4. Method statement
5. Primary CTA
6. Trust indicators
7. Course visual

Do not place a large image above the headline on mobile.

The headline and CTA should appear without excessive scrolling.

# 15. Mobile conversion experience

Most visitors are expected to arrive from mobile devices.

The mobile experience must be designed first, not treated as a smaller desktop version.

## Sticky bottom CTA

Implement a mobile-only sticky enrollment bar.

It should appear after the user scrolls beyond the hero CTA.

The bar contains:

Left:
- A compact course label.
- Optional price, only if the real price is supplied.

Right:
- `আজই শুরু করুন`

### Requirements

- Keep the bar compact.
- Use a solid background.
- Respect safe-area insets.
- Add bottom padding so it does not cover the footer.
- Hide it when the final enrollment CTA is visible.
- Do not show it while the hero CTA is already visible.
- Ensure it never covers important content or form controls.

Use `IntersectionObserver` to detect visibility of the hero CTA and final CTA.

Avoid unnecessary scroll event handlers.
